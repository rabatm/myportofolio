## Context

Commando Krav Maga Perpignan (CKM66) is a Krav Maga club based at the Les 5 Éléments centre in Perpignan, France. Classes are taught by Jérôme Luque, a Level 4 instructor.

## The need

The club summed up, in its own words, what wasn't working before:

- members had to call to book their classes;
- people missed classes because they forgot;
- progress went untracked;
- time was lost on paperwork.

## The solution

I built all three parts of the project: the club's website, the iOS and Android mobile app, and the club management side.

The website presents the club, its schedule, its prices and its articles, and it is built to be found locally.

The app, free of charge, answers the points the club raised: members book classes in real time instead of calling, a reminder arrives before each class, and everyone can track their progress.

The app is available on the [App Store](https://apps.apple.com/us/app/ckm66/id6753923165) and on [Google Play](https://play.google.com/store/apps/details?id=com.ckm66.myapp).

## Features

**The website**

- **Showcase**: schedule, prices and a journal of articles.
- **Local search**: the site describes the club with structured data.

**The app**

- **Real-time booking** of classes.
- **Automatic waiting list** when a class is full.
- **Cancellation** of a booking.
- **Reminders** before each class, plus club news.
- **More than 20 badges** for attendance and progress.
- **Leaderboard** between members.
- **Progress tracking**.
- **Offline mode**, with sync.
- **Accounts provided by the club**.

## Under the hood

- **Three parts, one point of contact**: website, mobile app and club management, all built by me.
- **iOS and Android**: the app is published on both stores.
- **Offline**: the app keeps working without a connection and syncs afterwards.
- **Structured data** on the website, for local search.
