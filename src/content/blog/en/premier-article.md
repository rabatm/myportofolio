## A path that didn't start with code

I spent about twenty years in systems administration, at Lafarge, Steria, the French Ministry of Health, and then LCL: operations, securing sensitive infrastructure, Level 3 support, automation. It's foundational work, where above all you learn to understand why things break before you fix them.

At some point, I wanted to build things rather than maintain them. That led me to software development: first as a freelancer, then by going back to the fundamentals at École 42 in Perpignan. Since then, I've taken on a string of hands-on engagements — legacy takeovers, business applications, training users on retail management software — always with the same approach I brought from infrastructure: understand the real need before writing a single line of code.

This summer, I was also a tutor during the École 42 Piscine (its intensive selection bootcamp): guiding other candidates through the same exercises I once had to solve myself, without ever handing them the answer, is a good way to measure how far I've come.

## Why this site

A resume lists jobs and skills. It doesn't show much of what you can actually do. This portfolio grew out of the opposite impulse: presenting real projects, with their constraints, their technical choices, and their results, rather than a list of keywords.

## The hardest part: knowing what to show

The hardest part wasn't technical. It was choosing what to show. I've worked on a lot of things — infrastructure, mobile apps for field work, back offices, integrations with legacy systems — and not all of it deserves a place here. Striking the right balance between the site's deliberately lighthearted tone (the retro theme, HAL-9000 answering your questions) and the seriousness of the work on display took more back-and-forth than any part of the code.

## The goal

I'm keeping every door open for what comes next: a freelance engagement, a full-time role, a collaboration on a project. Above all, this site is meant for that — to give anyone who'd like to work with me an accurate picture of what I can do.
